About the data
  • Arrests: NYPD Arrests Data (Historic), NYC Open Data — source. Precinct boundaries: NYC Open Data. Population: .
  • Arrests ≠ crimes: each record is an arrest made by police, not a confirmed offence, and counts reflect enforcement priorities as much as behaviour.
  • Arrest locations are recorded at mid-block / intersection level, not the exact address.
  • 4 arrests recorded in precincts without a boundary polygon are excluded from all views.
  • The density heat layer excludes 7,116 records without valid coordinates.
  • Per-10k rates use 2020 census population. Precinct 22 (Central Park) has 129 residents and is excluded from rates, ranking and outliers. Two precincts were created during the period: 121 (Jul 2013, from parts of 120 and 122) and 116 (2024-12-19, from parts of 105 and 113). They are shown as no data before creation and as a partial year in their first year (excluded from ranking and outliers then). Per-10k rates of 105 and 113 use their pre-split 2020 census population (199,218 and 135,221) for 2006–2024 and today's (post-split) population from 2025. Per-10k rates of 120/122 before 2014 are inflated, because their population is counted within today's (post-2013) boundaries.
  • Sex and age are missing for 52.8% of 2025 records; "All years" in Sex / Age mode uses 2006–2024 only.
